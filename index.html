Selected Projects

Smart Finance Automator (AI + NLP Integration)

A high-complexity financial ecosystem that eliminates manual data entry by interpreting banking notifications and voice commands in real-time.

The Architecture: The system uses MacroDroid to intercept bank notifications and a Telegram Bot for voice inputs. Data is funneled into Make.com, where Google Gemini 1.5 Flash acts as a digital accountant, extracting structured JSON from raw text. The system features an automated installment engine that projects future cash flows month-by-month into a relational Notion database.

  • AI: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (Advanced NLP Extraction)
  • Integration: Make.com (Webhooks, Routers & Iterators)
  • Database: Notion (Relational Architecture & Rollups)
  • Mobile: Android MacroDroid & Telegram Bot API

* Note: Demonstration video and images use a sandbox environment with fictional data for privacy.
